The PhysicsBit method:
Diagnose · Unlock · Execute

Every class, every problem, every explanation at PhysicsBit is built around a single three-stage method. It's not a shortcut — it's the thinking process that every good physicist uses, made explicit and teachable.

Stage 01

Diagnose

"What is this problem actually testing?"

Before touching a formula — read the problem as a physicist. Identify the concept or combination of concepts being tested.

Stage 02

Unlock

"How exactly do these concepts connect here?"

Find the precise relationship between the concepts in this specific problem. This is the click — and it's what no other coaching teaches.

Stage 03

Execute

"How do I translate the physics into maths?"

Only now do equations appear. When Stages 1 and 2 are done well, the maths follows naturally — you don't recall the formula, you derive it.
